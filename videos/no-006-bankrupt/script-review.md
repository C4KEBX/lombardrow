## What works
- The open has a death, a city and a motive in one 10-word sentence. Then it adds a hook into the present day. This is the right shape for this niche.
- The money trail gives the middle a question ("So where was the money?") and an object answer ("tied in a rag").
- "Hide it. / Hang for it." is a sound-off summary of the 1706 law that fits in six words.
- The hedges hold in the body: "officials said", "short, by the officials' count", "likely one of only 4", "could be wiped", "usually freed".
- "The rope failed." is a quotable compression of f-discharge.
- The final frame matches the cover and the first frame, which current loop guidance recommends.

## Accuracy flags
- **`meta.hookLine` and `meta.cover.line`: "Hanged for hiding £13,513"** is stronger than f-deficiency. The commissioners stated a *deficiency* of £13,513, and no fact says he hid that sum. The body is careful ("said he was 13,513 pounds short"), but the opening on-screen line is not. Rewrite: **"Hanged, £13,513 short"** (keep the "Hanged" mark).
- **`cut`: "a woman, Ferne" / "She kept the other halves." and kinetic "Half for her."** In f-cut-notes, "she" is the witness, Harris ("testified that she saw Ferne"). No fact says Ferne was a woman, so that needs a source. Rewrite at the same 21 words: **"A witness swore she saw Ferne cut banknotes in half and give one half to Perrott. Ferne kept the matching halves."** Change the lines to "Half for him." / "Half for Ferne." and move the "her" cue to "Ferne".
- **`fresh-start`: "that law's afterthought" / "The afterthought lasted"** drops "almost" from f-discharge. Fix 2 puts it back.
- **`hook`: "The same law gave bankrupts something they still get today."** f-1706-capital ties the 1706 act to both the capital crime and discharge. f-perrott-hanged says only that he was hanged "for fraudulent bankruptcy", and no fact says he was tried under that act itself. Safer at +1 word: **"The hanging law also gave bankrupts something they still get today."** Otherwise this needs a source for the statute Perrott was tried under.
- **`hanged`: "ever"** goes beyond the 1706 to 1820 window in f-four-hanged. Fix 1 removes it.
- Each of these needs a source before any line says it: why the notes were halved, who Ferne was to Perrott, how Perrott came to be in Newgate, and whether the notes formed part of the £13,513.
