## What works
- The trunk now proves the witness right. Order: the law, the witness, the search at Ferne's, the other halves in the trunk, the rope. That is the "follow the money" shape the plan asked for.
- The ending line and the first line share the same noun, "money", and the same frame, Tyburn. A clean loop like this earns rewatches.
- The cover line "Hanged, about £3m short in today's money" keeps "about" and "in today's money", and it gives the stakes at a glance.
- The hedges hold everywhere: "officials said", "short, by the officials' count", "A witness swore", "likely one of only 4", "could be wiped", "usually freed".
- `deal` and `fresh-start` pay off the hook promise ("a way out") with a number on screen, "12 / months to be freed, usually".

## Accuracy flags
- None in the narration as written. Each line traces to its fact: f-perrott-hanged, f-1706-capital, f-perrott-shop, f-deficiency, f-value-today, f-cut-notes (now "at Ferne's", which matches the source), f-half-notes, f-four-hanged, f-discharge and f-discharge-today. "A witness swore" for "testified" is fine, because trial testimony is given on oath. "in England" is narrower than "England and Wales", so it is still true.
- Watch the implied link. The script follows "So where was the money?" with the cut notes, but it never says the notes were the missing £13,513, and it must not. The source dates the five notes at Ferne's to February or March 1761, after the 1760 bankruptcy, and gives their total as £185. Any added line such as "the hidden money" or "his fortune, in halves" would be stronger than the source.
- The cover line "Hanged, about £3m short" drops "by the officials' count". That is acceptable, because the deficiency is the commissioners' stated figure. It could also be read as "hanged for being short", when he was hanged for fraudulent bankruptcy. Keep the in-video card's hedge.
- Fixes 1 and 2 each need a new entry in facts.json (f-newgate, f-ferne) before the lines are used. I read both on Credit Slips Part 3 on 2026-10-16.
