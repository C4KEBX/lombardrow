## What works
- The first frame and the last frame are now the same gallows. Together with "Hanged for hiding money", it is the clearest cover this video has had.
- The trunk line follows the source almost word for word and is easy to follow by ear: "tied in a rag", "matching halves", "half a 1,000 pound note".
- "The rope failed to make bankrupts honest. The deal lasted." is accurate (f-discharge) and quotable.
- The hedges hold: "officials said", "short, by the officials' count", "A witness swore", "likely one of only 4", "could be wiped", "usually freed", and "about, in today's money".
- `fresh-start` "In England" is narrower than the source's "England and Wales", so it is still true.

## Accuracy flags
- **On-screen text stronger than its line:** `deal` "Cooperate. / Debts wiped." drops "could be". Next to `fresh-start`, it can also read as present-day debt advice (fix 3).
- **Credits that make a claim:** `cut` "Searchers ... at Ferne's" over "Thomas Morris, Ludgate Hill, London." suggests Ferne's was on Ludgate Hill, and no fact says so. `trunk` "rag" over "In the Fleet Prison" contradicts "Newgate prison" (f-half-notes). See fix 1 for both.
- **Watch: don't tighten "the hanging law" into "the law that hanged him".** f-1706-capital says the 1706 act made hiding assets a capital crime and introduced the discharge. f-perrott-hanged says he was hanged for fraudulent bankruptcy in 1761. Nothing in facts.json says he was tried under the 1706 act itself. "The hanging law" (as written) is fine. "The law that hanged him" or "the same law still frees bankrupts" **needs a source**.
- **Implied link:** "So where was the money?" is followed by the cut notes. Nothing says the notes were the missing £13,513. Don't add "the missing money" or "his fortune" anywhere on screen.
- **Hook line and cover, "Hanged for hiding money":** acceptable. f-1706-capital defines the crime as "hiding assets from creditors", and the evidence was banknotes. Don't make it "for hiding £13,513".
- **Credit dates:** `hanged` "In 1761, Perrott was hanged at Smithfield" sits over "William Hogarth, a London hanging, 1747". The credit is honest and doesn't name Smithfield (assets.json: "not Perrott, and not Smithfield"), so no change is needed.
- **Needs a source** before use: who Ferne was, why Perrott was in Newgate, and today's value of the £1,000 note.
