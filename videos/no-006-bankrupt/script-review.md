## What works
- The cover, the first frame and the last frame are the same gallows, and "Hanged for hiding money" proves itself with the sound off.
- `trunk` now plays as a real reveal. "Perrott was in Newgate prison." lands on Newgate, "tied in a rag:" lands on a torn note, and "half a 1,000 pound note" pulls out wider on the same tear.
- The hedges all hold: "officials said", "short, by the officials' count", "A witness swore", "likely one of only 4", "could be wiped", "usually freed", and "about, in today's money".
- "The rope failed to make bankrupts honest. The deal lasted." is accurate (f-discharge) and quotable.
- `hook` "way" on the Fleet's barred window, under "a way out", is a good match of picture and words.

## Accuracy flags
- **Image credit, on screen:** `boe-note` "Bank of England £70 note" puts a sum that no fact mentions over "1,000", "13,513" and "3 million" (fix 1). The credit is true of the image but misleading where it sits.
- **Image credit, implied place:** `short` "short" on `rake-fleet` ("In the Fleet Prison") under Perrott's shortfall suggests a prison that no fact links to him. f-half-notes puts him in Newgate (fix 1).
- **Watch, Newgate picture (outside facts.json):** the 1780 riot print most likely shows George Dance's rebuilt Newgate, begun in 1770, not the older building Perrott was held in. The credit "Newgate prison, 1781" and the title "Newgate prison, London" are honest, so no change is needed. Don't change either to "where Perrott was held". `assets.json` "depicts" ("used for the prison where he was held") slightly overstates it. Also check in preview that the man climbing the ladder and the "No Popery" flag (about x0.63, y0.53 to 0.62) stay out of the opener crop x0.61 y0.47 w0.1. By my calculation the crop reaches y≈0.62, so they are in the lower third.
- **Kinetic text "Hang for it.":** acceptable as a gloss of "a hanging crime" (f-1706-capital). Don't make it stronger, for example "You'd hang", because f-four-hanged says likely only four were hanged.
- **Implied link:** nothing says the cut notes were the missing £13,513. Keep "So where was the money?" as a question. Don't caption "the missing money" or "his fortune".
- **"The hanging law":** fine as written. "The law that hanged him" **needs a source** (no fact says which act he was tried under).
- **Needs a source** before use: who Ferne was, today's value of the £1,000 note, and the terms of a 1760 discharge.
- Narration: no line is stronger than its fact.
