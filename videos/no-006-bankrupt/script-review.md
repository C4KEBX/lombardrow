## What works
- The hook line is true, deadly and plain, and it is the same in the narration, in the on-screen hook and in the cover from frame one.
- "One London merchant did" pays off as "That merchant was John Perrott, of Ludgate Hill." That is a clean loop, closed about halfway through.
- The motif runs "hid / hides / Hide it. / Hang for it. / hid one more idea". It ties the word, the law and the crime together.
- "And that law hid one more idea" sets up the twist about 30 s before "That other idea?" pays it off.
- The hedges are kept in `myth` ("Legend says", "probably") and `four` ("likely one of only 4"), and "Almost an afterthought" comes straight from Kadens.

## Accuracy flags
- `short`, "he came up 13,513 pounds short": f-deficiency is the commissioners' interrogatory *stating* a deficiency, which is a charge put to him, not an established finding. The last draft had "said"; this draft drops it. Put the attribution back (fix 3). The card label "short in Perrott's books" is stronger than the source, because no source mentions his books.
- `myth`: f-bench-myth is marked disputed. Etymonline says "probably figurative", while the Gazette has "the bench was broken in a symbolic show of failure". "The dictionary says" attributes the claim correctly. Keep it that way, and don't let it become "it never happened".
- `hanged` and `trunk`: the pictures must not read as Smithfield 1761 or Newgate 1760 (fix 2). The narration matches f-perrott-hanged and f-half-notes.
- `fresh-start`, "The discharge lasted": this is at the limit of f-discharge ("would have a fruitful future"). "Still exists today" or "a fresh start" (the wording in plan.json's angle) would need a source.
- `trunk`: why Perrott was in Newgate, and where the first halves of the notes were, both need a source.
- The facts disagree on a date. f-1542 says death came "by 1705", while f-1706-capital and the script say 1706. Keep 1706, which is Kadens's date for the Act of 4 & 5 Anne.
- verify.json could not reach the source for f-1706-capital, f-perrott-hanged, f-four-hanged or f-banca-rotta (HTTP 403). Archive copies are listed. Check the "4" and "1820" against the Kadens PDF before building.
